The Peer Project // About Us

The Peer Project is a completely free, student-led, and non-profit mentoring initiative. We connect high school students with seniors who have recently navigated the exact same academic challenges, competitive prep schedules, and board exams.

Our Origin Story

A few weeks ago, this was just an idea.

During our own board and competitive exams (like JEE and NEET), we realized there is a massive gap in how students are supported. While coaching institutes handle the raw syllabus and textbooks, nobody really teaches you the hard, daily mechanics of being a student:

  • How to build and stick to a realistic daily routine.
  • How to clear a mountain of backlogs without falling behind in current classes.
  • How to manage constant screen distractions and stay consistent.
  • How to handle the mental burnout and anxiety when mock test scores drop.

We wished we had access to seniors who had just finished the same exams - people who understood the exact pressure we were under and could offer honest, practical advice.

Since that support system did not exist, we decided to build it ourselves. We started as a small community initiative on Reddit (across r/CBSE, r/ICSE, and r/JEE), and within a week of launching, we received over 50 applications. Today, our first cohort is live on Discord.

Our Mission

Our mission is to bridge the gap between confusion and clarity for every student, regardless of their financial background.

We believe that the best guidance does not come from high-priced consultants or automated study planners; it comes from peers who have walked the path just a step ahead of you.

Our Core Values

  • 100% Free and Volunteer-Driven: We will never charge money for mentorship. TPP is run entirely by student volunteers who want to give back to the communities that supported them. There are no paywalls, premium tiers, or hidden fees.
  • Guide, Not Teacher: Our mentors do not teach raw syllabus concepts or write your school essays. Instead, they focus on the strategy of studying - sharing resources, managing backlogs, mapping time tables, and holding you accountable to your own goals.
  • Asynchronous and Low-Pressure: We know how busy a student's schedule is. By using asynchronous text communication on Discord, we ensure that both mentors and mentees can connect, check in, and respond at their own pace without the pressure of live scheduling.
  • Privacy and Safety First: We prioritize student safety. Mentees and mentors interact under display names of their choice, and all communication is kept strictly within private, moderated Discord channels to ensure personal contact details are never shared.
