How It Works

  1. 1.   apply via the form below and tell us your goals / need
  2. 2.   get matched with a verified peer mentor
  3. 3.   async text check-ins on Discord - respond within 24-48 hours at your own pace
  4. 4.   voice calls optional, only if both sides agree.
Privacy Note: Your real name is never shared with your mentor. You interact under a display name of your choice throughout.
