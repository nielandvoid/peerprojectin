The Peer Project // Mentee Guide

Welcome to The Peer Project. You have been paired with a senior mentor who has volunteered their time to help you navigate your academic journey - someone who has walked a path very similar to yours and wants to make yours a little less confusing.

Here is a quick guide on what to expect and how to get the most out of it.

What to Expect

  • Private Channel: You will be added to a private channel on Discord with your mentor. Only you, your mentor, and the TPP admin team can see this channel.
  • Text-Based, Asynchronous: All communication happens through text. Your mentor will check in with you regularly, but they will reply when they are free - not instantly. This is not a tutoring service or a helpline, it is a mentorship.
  • A Guide, Not a Tutor: Your mentor is here to help you with strategy, study habits, approach, resources, and honest advice. They are not here to teach you chapters or solve your homework. Think of them as the senior you wish you had access to.

How to Make the Most of It

  • Be Specific: Instead of saying "I struggle with physics," try something like "I am having a hard time with numericals in kinematics and need a better approach." The more specific you are, the more useful the advice you get back.
  • Be Honest: If you did not follow through on the goals you set together, just say so. Your mentor has been in your shoes and they understand. Honesty helps them adjust the plan to actually fit you - not some ideal version of you.
  • Take Ownership: Your mentor can guide you, but they cannot study for you. The effort has to come from your side. Come to each check-in with something to share - even if it is just "I tried X and it did not work."
  • Respect Their Time: Your mentor is a student or a recent passout. They have their own academics, exams, and commitments. Be mindful of that, and be polite.

Privacy and Safety

  • Keep It Inside Discord: Do not share your real name, school name, phone number, or personal social media handles with your mentor. All communication stays inside your private Discord channel.
  • Be Respectful: Keep all language polite and constructive. Any form of harassment or inappropriate behavior will lead to immediate removal from the project.
  • Reach Out If Needed: If you ever feel uncomfortable, or if your mentor has been completely unresponsive for over a week, let the admin team know. We are here to help.

Getting Started

Your private channel will be set up on Wednesday.

  1. Say Hello: When it is ready, drop a message introducing yourself - at your own pace. There is no rush.
  2. Share What You Need Help With First: Give your mentor a brief idea of your most immediate need (a specific subject, a study schedule, university planning, etc.).
  3. Start Small: Do not try to fix everything on day one. Pick one thing to work on this week and build from there.

We are glad to have you here. Make the most of it.
