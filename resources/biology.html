Boards & NEET

Campbell is the foundation for boards and NEET alike - any edition from the ninth onwards is fine. (JEE does not test biology, which is why it is not in this track's name.)

Olympiad (INBO / IBO)

The exams test reasoning rather than recall - Campbell is necessary but not sufficient. Everything below builds on it, and only after it.

Past papers

Timed past papers matter more in biology than in almost any other subject - olympiad biology tests data interpretation and experimental reasoning, which you cannot build by reading alone.

USABO Open and Semifinal papers are free and worth the same timed practice as the Indian and international papers above.
