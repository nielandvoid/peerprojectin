ICSE / ISC

We will be honest here: free, well-organised ICSE and ISC material is genuinely harder to find than its CBSE equivalent. This is a real gap rather than an oversight on our part. Selina and Frank solutions are the usual fallback, and ICSE mentees tend to lean on their mentor more heavily than CBSE ones as a result.

If you have found something good for ICSE or ISC, please tell us - we would like this section to be less thin than it is.
