The Peer Project // Law & Management Entrances

CLAT, AILET and IPMAT reward reading speed and reasoning, not how much you've memorised. The prep looks nothing like entrance-exam prep.

Reading & Comprehension

Every one of these exams is a reading-speed test. Building genuine reading stamina outperforms any shortcut.

AeonFree

Long-form essays on philosophy, science and society, written by actual specialists rather than content mills. Read one or two a day and you build both vocabulary and the stamina for dense passages, which is exactly what CLAT's English and legal reasoning sections are testing.

Smithsonian MagazineFree

Slightly more accessible than Aeon and a good place to start if dense philosophy essays feel like too much too soon. History, science and culture writing at a level that still stretches you.

Flesch-Kincaid CalculatorFree

Paste in a passage and it scores the reading grade level required to understand it. Useful for checking whether the passage that just wrecked you was actually hard, or whether you have a specific gap to work on - IPMAT passages typically score 10-13 on this scale, meaning early college level.

Logical Reasoning

M.K. Pandey - Analytical Reasoning

The standard starting point for logical reasoning theory. Builds the underlying concepts - syllogisms, analytical decision making, input-output - before throwing practice questions at you, which matters if you do not already know what the topics are called.

Test Series // Video Lectures

None of these are free end-to-end, but each has a genuinely useful free layer - YouTube lecture series, sectional tests, or both - worth using before paying for anything.

Legal Edge

CLAT-focused, with a large free YouTube library of one-shots and full lecture series alongside their paid mock series. A reasonable moderate-difficulty mock benchmark, close to actual CLAT PYQ difficulty.

Law Prep Tutorial

One of the older, larger names in CLAT coaching, with free live classes and lecture series on YouTube alongside their paid test series. Good for building topic-wise foundations before you start timed practice.

CLAT WallahFree

Free sectional tests and live/recorded lecture series, run by an NLU Patiala graduate. A solid free-only option if you are not ready to pay for a full test series yet.

Communities

r/CLATFree

Active community of aspirants and current NLU students, with a strong culture of honest, unsponsored advice and occasional AMAs with teachers and toppers. Good for the questions a coaching institute has no incentive to answer honestly, like whether a given cutoff prediction is nonsense.

PaGaLGuYFree

India's oldest management-entrance forum, running since 2002. Built around CAT rather than IPMAT specifically, but IPMAT aspirants weighing it against CAT down the line will find genuinely experienced voices here that a CLAT-only community will not have.

Law Prep Tutorial (Telegram)Free

Faster-moving than the YouTube channel above - current-affairs drops, doubt-clearing and schedule announcements land here first.

We do not have a strong standalone IPMAT-specific community to recommend - most of what exists is either dead or thinly disguised coaching-institute marketing. If you know a genuinely good one, tell us.

a note on past papers

Unlike JEE or the boards, neither the Consortium of NLUs (CLAT), NLU Delhi (AILET) nor IIM Indore (IPMAT) officially publishes a public archive of past papers. What circulates online is reconstructed or third-party, not authoritative - if you find copies through a mentor or test series, treat them as a rough guide rather than a guaranteed match for the real thing.
