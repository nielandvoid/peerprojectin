How to use this

Most resource lists are a pile of links with no sense of who they are for. That is the part we have tried to fix. Where a book is brilliant but brutal, we say so. Where something is genuinely the right starting point, we say that too.

There are two browsing options. Go by exam if you know what you are preparing for. Go by subject if you would rather browse a subject directly.

a note on sourcing

We link to original and legal sources only - official textbook portals, university course pages, open-access textbooks, and past papers from the exam bodies themselves. We do not host copyrighted books, and we ask our mentors and mentees not to circulate pirated material in sessions.

This is partly principle and partly practical: the free, legal material available today is genuinely good enough that nobody needs to go looking elsewhere.

found something broken?

Links move and tools change. If something here is dead, paywalled, or simply bad advice for a student like you, tell us at contact@peerproject.in and we will fix it.
