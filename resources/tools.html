The goodstuff.

Reference sites

Things to look things up in.

HyperPhysicsFree

Georgia State. Physics organised as a clickable concept map rather than chapters - every page links to the ideas above and below it. Unbeatable for 'I half-remember this formula, where does it come from?'. The design is stuck in 1999 and it remains the best of its kind.

Paul's Online Math NotesFree

Lamar University. Complete notes for algebra, calculus I-III and differential equations, plus practice problems with full worked solutions and downloadable cheat sheets. The most useful free maths reference on the internet at this level.

LibreTextsFree

Full open-access university textbooks across chemistry, physics, biology, mathematics and statistics.

OpenStaxFree

Free, peer-reviewed, properly published textbooks from Rice University. No signup.

Wolfram MathWorldFree

The most comprehensive mathematics encyclopedia online. More rigorous and more complete than Wikipedia for mathematical definitions.

OEIS - Online Encyclopedia of Integer SequencesFree

Type in the first few terms of a sequence and get its identity, formula and references. Essential for olympiad combinatorics and number theory, and almost nobody at school level knows it exists.

EqWorldFree

Russian Academy of Sciences. Extremely niche: a catalogue of exact solutions to ODEs, PDEs, integral and functional equations. For when you need to know whether a closed form exists at all.

AoPS WikiFree

Olympiad-specific theorem and technique reference, written by competition students for competition students.

Cut the KnotFree

A huge archive of mathematical proofs, puzzles and interactive demonstrations. Famous for collecting well over a hundred distinct proofs of the Pythagorean theorem.

Interactive tools

Things to play with.

PhET SimulationsFree

University of Colorado Boulder, free and open source. Research-based simulations across physics, chemistry, biology and maths. Lets you see the invisible - fields, wave interference, atomic behaviour, circuits. If a concept is not landing, this is usually the fastest fix.

DesmosFree

A graphing calculator genuinely better than most paid software. Parameter sliders make it excellent for 'what happens to the curve when I change this?'

GeoGebraFree

Dynamic geometry, algebra and calculus in one tool. For olympiad geometry specifically, dragging a construction to see which properties stay invariant is a real problem-solving technique, not just a visual aid.

TrackerFree

Free and open source video analysis. Film a real experiment on your phone and Tracker extracts position, velocity and acceleration frame by frame. Genuinely useful for board practicals and projects, and almost nobody uses it.

PtableFree

Interactive periodic table with properties, electron configurations, orbitals and isotopes layered on top. Far better than the static one at the back of your textbook.

MolViewFree

Draw a molecule in 2D, see it rendered in 3D instantly. Very good for organic stereochemistry, where the entire difficulty is spatial.

NIST Chemistry WebBookFree

US National Institute of Standards and Technology. Authoritative thermochemical, spectroscopic and physical property data - this is where the real numbers come from.

PubChemFree

NIH database of over a hundred million compounds with structures, properties and safety data.

Wolfram AlphaFree tier

Computational answers to almost anything. Use it to check a final answer. Using it instead of working the problem will quietly destroy your ability to solve anything unfamiliar, which is exactly what exams test.

Study and accountability tools

AnkiFree

Open source spaced repetition, using the modern FSRS scheduling algorithm. Steeper learning curve than most flashcard apps, and worth it. Shared decks for JEE and NEET already exist on AnkiWeb.

YPT (Yeolpumta)Free

Subject-wise time tracking with live study groups and rankings. What most students mean when they say they are 'on YPT'.

YPT-WebFree

An open-source, browser-based version of YPT's core timer. No install, no account.

JoplinFree

Open source note-taking with Markdown, encryption and a built-in to-do mode. Syncs through your own cloud storage, so there is no vendor lock-in.

ObsidianFree

Markdown notes with a graph view for linking concepts. Useful if you are building a connected revision system rather than isolated notes per chapter.
