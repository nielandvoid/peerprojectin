The Peer Project // How It Works

The Peer Project is a free, student-run mentorship initiative that connects high school and prep students (mentees) with seniors (mentors) who have recently walked the same academic path.

Our goal is not to teach you the syllabus, but to help you navigate the journey.

Overall Structure

  • The Workspace: Everything takes place in private text channels on our Discord server. Only the mentor, the mentee, and the TPP admin team have access.
  • Asynchronous & Text-Based: Mentorship is fully text-based. You don't need to schedule live video calls or be online at the same time. You drop messages and reply whenever you are free.
  • Duration: A mentoring cycle runs for 8 to 12 weeks, matching key phases of the academic term or exam preparation schedules.

For Mentees

Your mentor is a senior volunteer who wants to make your path a little less confusing.

  • A Guide, Not a Tutor: Your mentor will not teach you chapters or solve your homework. They are here to help you figure out how to study, where to find the best resources, and how to structure your routine.
  • Take Ownership: Your mentor can guide you, but they cannot study for you. Come to your check-ins with updates on what you tried - even if it didn’t work.
  • Be Specific & Honest: Instead of saying "I struggle with physics," try something like "I am having a hard time with numericals in kinematics." If you did not follow through on a goal, just say so. Honesty helps your mentor adjust the plan to fit the *real* you.
  • Privacy First: Keep all communication inside Discord. Do not share phone numbers, real names, or personal social media handles.

For Mentors

You are the backbone of this project. By volunteering your time, you help bridge the gap between confusion and clarity.

  • Matching Stage: We match you with a mentee based on your academic background, target tracks (Boards, JEE/NEET, Abroad), and shared interests.
  • The Weekly Rhythm: We recommend a lightweight, flexible check-in routine:
    Goal Setting: At the start of the week, help your mentee set 2-3 realistic academic goals.
    Mid-Week Check-in: Send a quick text to see if they're facing blockers.
    Weekly Sync: Review their progress together at the end of the week.
  • You are a Guide, Not a Teacher: Your job is to listen, share your own struggles/successes, and guide their approach - not to teach raw concepts or write essays.
  • Protect Your Boundaries: You are volunteering your time. Set clear expectations early on about when you check Discord and how quickly you'll respond.
